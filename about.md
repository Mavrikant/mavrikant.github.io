Hi, I'm **M. Serdar Karaman**. I live in Ankara, Türkiye, and work as a senior avionics software design engineer. I develop safety-critical software to DO-178C DAL A, the assurance level for software whose failure could be catastrophic. I started writing embedded software in 2016, while still at university, and I've been in avionics since 2019.

Most of my days revolve around requirements, C code written to MISRA C, Xilinx Zynq-based hardware, ARINC 429 and MIL-STD-1553 buses, and the test automation that checks it all. I also write DO-178C plans, standards and design documents, review code and documents together with my teammates, and try to help interns and new engineers find their feet. Before my current role, I worked on cockpit display software for a helicopter, swarm-UAV simulation and management software, and a portable ECG device.

I studied Electronics and Communication Engineering at İzmir Institute of Technology, then did a master's in Software Engineering at Hacettepe University. Over time I've also earned two certifications in software testing and one in systems engineering:

- ISTQB Certified Tester Foundation Level (CTFL)
- ISTQB Certified Tester, Advanced Level Test Automation Engineer (CTAL-TAE)
- INCOSE Certified Systems Engineering Professional (CSEP): I wrote about [the road to it]({% post_url 2026-10-01-incose-csep-sertifikasyonu-yolculugum %}) (in Turkish)

Preparing for CSEP showed me how far the field reaches beyond the INCOSE handbook, and that's one of the reasons I'm now doing a second master's in systems engineering.

Outside work, I write here and on [aviyonikyazilim.com](https://www.aviyonikyazilim.com/), an open-source Turkish book on DO-178C; corrections and contributions there are always welcome on GitHub. I've been volunteering on Wikipedia since 2014. Together with a bot I wrote, I've made more than 220,000 edits across Wikimedia projects, most of them on Turkish Wikipedia, where I also help look after the site's interface. My side projects are on [GitHub](https://github.com/Mavrikant), and a couple of them live here: [Games](https://karaman.dev/games) and [OR Tools](https://karaman.dev/or-araclari).
